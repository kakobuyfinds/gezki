Check a listing before ordering

  1. Open the HipobuyFind listing and use its Buy Link options to locate the original seller listing, when available.
  2. Match your size and color against the seller’s options. Compare the size chart with measurements from an item you already own.
  3. If you plan to use iTaoBuy, confirm it accepts the original seller link and can ship the item to your destination. Check its current fees and return terms before paying.
  4. If warehouse quality-control photos are available, inspect the size tag, color and condition before approving shipment. Use a quote based on the packed parcel weight and dimensions when available.
What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping research list that groups product references so you can compare items and prices. This page uses HipobuyFind listings as its product source.

Where do the two shopping buttons go?

Both open the HipobuyFind homepage. To research a specific product, use its link in the catalog and check the seller and purchasing options there.

What goes into the estimated total?

The planner adds the listed prices for your selected items and the costs you enter. Update your estimate after checking variant prices, shipping quotes and any destination charges.
